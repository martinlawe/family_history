Family History

This page provides a brief overview as to where the project currently stands, with a brief view of the current research projects being undertaken.

Where are we now, what do we currently know, and what to investigate.

The Family genealogy has two main areas of investigation.

The Irish Tree

The Irish tree, which is not as straightforward to investigate as one would expect. Currently there are two lines of enquiry.

  1. The first line was looking at the O'Keefe line. This took us back to 1850 and the Griffith's Valuation file, which showed that the house and land in Smithstown, Upper, was tenanted by John Keeffe in 1850. The plot is designated specifically within the Griffith's Valuation Map, and so from there we could plot the inhabitants of the house, and also the movement of some family members as they moved from the property to Jackstown.

    This is where John Keeffe lived with his family whilst working for the Butler family at Kilmurry, and then back again. This has always been a difficult search due to the number of Keeffe's, of various spellings, who lived in the Thomastown area.

    I also touched on the Kennedy side of the family, but this came to a stop when again I was faced with a number of Kennedys with the same occupation.

    1. There are a few offshoots that I could investigate, but I'm not sure of the value in doing so, unless there is a good reason to do so.

  2. The second line of research in Ireland was the Lawless arm. This has proven to be interesting, in that again we can get back to 1850 through the Griffith's Valuation file, which identified a number of plots of land in Killeen that were of interest, such as a plot tenanted by John Lawless, and also a number tenanted by the Knox family.

    This has resulted in a map of who was where and when for Killeen being sorted. There are still a number of loose ends to be resolved here, and currently I am waiting for the National Archives of Ireland website to come back up.

The UK Tree

The Lawrence Family

This has been an interesting research project. I have identified the US side of the family, but that is all. Researching the UK side takes us back into the Emmons family, and back to the late 1700s. The Spiers family takes us back into the early 1800s, but again we seem to have hit a stop.

The Archers are not a blood relation, so I am not going to research that line.

The Vickers Family

This has been an interesting research project, which has split into a number of sub-projects.

  • The D'Oyly Family — This goes back to the 12th Century, and has identified a number of transactions between the D'Oyly family and St Mary's Church in Eynsham.
  • The Brocas Family — This goes back again to the 12th Century. I believe this line of research has been completed, and does join the D'Oyly family before moving to the Vickers family.
  • WW1 Research — The WW1 research sub-project has been interesting, and there is still work to be done around mapping etc.
  • History of the Vickers Family — This has required some untangling from data gleaned from FamilySearch, which may not be completely accurate.

I have made contact with and had a response from the Berkshire Archives. I needed to see the marriage entry for William Vickers and Sarah Gibbins in Clewer in 1826. The records are being published through Ancestry in mid-September, so a visit to the local library is called for towards the end of the month. The aim is to get a copy of the record, and then we can see where to go from there.
